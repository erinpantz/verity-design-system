# Tooltip

A short hint that appears while someone hovers over or focuses a control, such as “Coming soon” on a link to a page that isn’t live yet. Hover over or tab to the button below to see it.

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Default} />

## When to use

- **Through the component that owns it.** `Link` shows one when it’s disabled, and `IconButton` always shows its label in one. You rarely place a Tooltip yourself.
- **For a few words** that people can do without: a page that’s coming soon, or the name of an icon-only control.
- **Not for anything people need to read to use the page,** like instructions or errors. Put that text on the page, where everyone sees it, including people on touch screens.
- **Not on something that can’t be focused.** Keyboard users would never see it.

## Accessibility

A Tooltip renders a `<span role="tooltip">` next to its trigger. It follows WCAG 1.4.13, Content on hover or focus:

- **It appears** when the trigger has keyboard focus, and when the pointer is over the trigger on a device with a real pointer (a mouse or trackpad). It doesn’t appear after a mouse click, or after a tap on a touch screen, where it would stay stuck on screen.
- **It stays** while the pointer moves onto the tooltip itself, so people can read it at any zoom.
- **Escape hides it** without moving the pointer or focus. It comes back on the next hover or focus.

The trigger has to point to the tooltip by its `id`, so screen readers read it:

| Situation | What to do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| The tooltip adds a hint | Point to it with `aria-describedby`. Screen readers read the trigger’s name, then the hint. | A disabled Link: “Case studies, Coming soon” |
| The tooltip is the trigger’s only name | Point to it with `aria-labelledby`, so its text is the name and isn’t read twice. | An IconButton: “Collapse navigation” |
| The tooltip repeats a name screen readers already get, like a visually hidden label | Set `visualOnly`. The tooltip is hidden from screen readers, so they don’t hear the name twice. | A NavItem in the collapsed rail: “Portfolio” |
| The text matters on a phone | Don’t use a tooltip. Touch screens have no hover. | Instructions for a form field |

## Placement

| `placement` | Where it sits | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `top` | The default. Above the trigger, lined up with its start. | `Link` |
| `top-end` | Above the trigger, lined up with its end, so it doesn’t spill past whatever the trigger sits at the end of | `Animation`’s pause and play button |
| `end` | Beside the trigger, after it, centered on it. If it wouldn’t fit on the screen, it opens below the trigger instead, with its end lined up with the trigger’s end. | `IconButton`, `ThemeToggle` |

The gap between the trigger and the tooltip is `--verity-tooltip-offset` (8) in every placement. It leaves room for the trigger’s focus ring, so the ring never touches the tooltip.

## Space

Inside, the tooltip has `--verity-tooltip-padding-y` (4) above and below its text and `--verity-tooltip-padding-x` (8) on each side. Its text stays on one line. It floats over the page, so it adds no space around its trigger.

## Color

The tooltip uses the knockout colors, the opposite of the page: `--verity-color-background-knockout` behind `--verity-color-content-knockout` text, in the label-sm text style. Its corners use `--verity-border-radius-sm`.
