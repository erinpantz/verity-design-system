# NavCard

A large, square link to one of the site’s pages, with the page’s nav icon above its name. On hover, the icon moves just as it does in the nav.

<Canvas of={NavCardStories.Default} />

## When to use

- **On Home, as a playful way into the site’s main pages,** two or more in a `NavCardGroup`, which overlaps and tilts them.
- **For the Portfolio and Profile pages,** which have their own nav icons.
- **Not for navigation that’s always there.** That’s `MainNav`, with `NavItem`s.
- **Not for a piece of work.** Use `PortfolioCard`.

## Accessibility

Its link goes through `LinkProvider`, so with a site’s router, it changes the page in the app (see **Actions › LinkProvider**).

A NavCard renders one `<a href>` around everything, so the whole card is one stop for the keyboard and one link for screen readers.

- **Its name is its label:** a screen reader hears “Portfolio, link.”
- **The icon is decorative,** hidden from screen readers: the label already says where it goes.
- **The tilt and the motion don’t change what it means.** With reduce motion on, it doesn’t lift, grow, or straighten.

| Situation | What to do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Writing the label | The page’s name, as in the nav, so the two match | “Portfolio” |
| Choosing `page` | The page it goes to, so its icon matches the nav’s | `page="profile"` |

## Tilt

| `tilt` | The card |
|---|---|
| `none` | The default. Straight. |
| `left` | Turned `--verity-nav-card-tilt` (4) degrees to the left (counterclockwise) |
| `right` | Turned 4 degrees to the right (clockwise) |

On hover and focus a tilted card straightens and comes in front of its neighbor. In a `NavCardGroup`, the group sets each card’s tilt and decides which card is on top, so don’t set `tilt` there. See **Navigation › NavCardGroup**.

## States

| State | What changes | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| Default | The brand fill and border, the default text color, a small shadow, and the icon in its own color | `--verity-color-background-brand`, `--verity-color-border-brand`, `--verity-color-content-default`, `--verity-shadow-sm-*` |
| Hover | It rises, grows a little, straightens, and comes in front, with a large shadow, a lighter fill and border, and brighter text. The icon takes its hover pose and accent colors. Devices with a real pointer only, so a tap doesn’t leave it raised. | `--verity-motion-lift-md` (8), `--verity-motion-scale-hover` (1.02), `--verity-shadow-lg-*`, `--verity-color-background-brand-hover`, `--verity-color-border-brand-hover`, `--verity-color-content-default-hover` |

| Active | It settles back down with the small shadow, so pressing reads as pushing it in, and a darker fill and border. It stays straight, and the icon keeps its hover pose. | `--verity-color-background-brand-active`, `--verity-color-border-brand-active`, `--verity-shadow-sm-*` |
| Disabled | None: a NavCard always goes somewhere | — |

## Icon

The icon is the page’s nav icon, from the shared `NavIcon`, so it has the same poses as in the nav: on hover and focus it moves to its hover pose and takes its accent colors, and keeps them while pressed. Where the nav uses `--verity-nav-icon-color-accent-brand`, NavCard uses its own `--verity-nav-card-icon-color-accent-brand`, which shows on the brand fill.

| What | Token |
|---|---|
| Size | `--verity-nav-card-icon-size` (96). The strokes keep their width, as in every icon. |
| Brand accent on hover | `--verity-nav-card-icon-color-accent-brand` |

## Motion

| What | How | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| The lift, growth, and straightening | On hover and focus, and back on mouse out, blur, or press (it stays straight while pressed) | `--verity-motion-duration-playful`, `--verity-motion-easing-standard` |
| Fill, border, text color, and shadow | Fade between states | `--verity-motion-duration-fast`, `--verity-motion-easing-standard` |
| The icon’s pose | As in the nav | `--verity-motion-duration-playful`, `--verity-motion-easing-playful` |
| Reduce motion is on | No lift, growth, or straightening, and a tilted card stays tilted. The fades stay, and the icon switches pose at once. | — |

## Space

| Space | Token |
|---|---|
| The card’s width and height | `--verity-nav-card-size` (200) |
| Inside the card | `--verity-padding-sm` |
| Between the icon and the label | `--verity-spacing-xxs` |
| Corners and border | `--verity-border-radius-md`, `--verity-border-width-sm` |
| The label | `label-default` |

It adds no space outside itself: the stack it’s in sets that.
