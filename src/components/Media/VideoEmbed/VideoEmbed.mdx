# VideoEmbed

A video hosted elsewhere, like Vimeo, with sound, the provider’s own controls, and full screen. It shows a poster with a play button until someone presses it, and only then loads the player.

<Canvas of={VideoEmbedStories.Default} />

## When to use

- **For a video with sound, or longer than a short clip,** like a talk, an interview, or a full walkthrough with narration.
- **With a caption,** put it in a `MediaBlock`, as its media, the same way as an Image or a Video.
- **Not for a short, silent clip we host,** like a 10-second prototype walkthrough. Use a `Video`: it loops on its own, with no player to load and nothing sent to another site.
- **Not when a still says it all.** Use an `Image`.

## Accessibility

Before it’s pressed, the whole poster is one `<button>`, named “Play” plus the video’s `title`, so screen readers hear “Play The new Vimeo player, button.” The poster’s `alt` is read as its description. The play button on it is only a look (`IconButton` with `as="span"`), so there’s one thing to press, not two.

Pressing it replaces the poster with the provider’s player, an `<iframe>` named by `title`, and moves keyboard focus into the player, so keyboard users can carry on with its controls. The player brings its own controls, captions, and full screen.

| Situation | What to do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Writing the title | The video’s own title, as on the provider, without “Video” | `title="Designing AI that care teams trust"` |
| Writing the alt | What the poster shows, in a sentence. Use `alt=""` when the title says enough. | `alt="Erin presenting at a whiteboard"` |
| The video has speech | Turn on captions on the provider: they show in its player (WCAG 1.2.2) | Vimeo’s captions, uploaded with the video |

### Click to load

Nothing loads from the provider until someone presses play: no player, no request, and no cookies. The page stays light, and a visitor who never plays the video never contacts Vimeo. The player then starts on its own, with `autoplay`, since the visitor just asked for it, and Vimeo is asked not to track them (`dnt=1`). If the browser won’t start a video with sound by itself, Vimeo starts it muted, with its Unmute button showing.

Nothing moves or plays before the press, so there’s nothing extra to do for reduce motion.

## Provider

| `provider` | The player | Video ID |
|---|---|---|
| `vimeo` | The default. `https://player.vimeo.com/video/<id>?autoplay=1&dnt=1`, with autoplay, full screen, picture-in-picture, and encrypted media allowed. Vimeo needs encrypted media to start playing: without it, its player shows “Unable to play video.” | The number at the end of the video’s link: `76979871` in `vimeo.com/76979871` |

Only Vimeo for now. Another provider is one more entry in the component’s list of players.

## Aspect ratio

| `aspectRatio` | Use for |
|---|---|
| `16:9` | The default: most videos |
| `3:2` | To match photos and Video clips around it |
| `4:3` | Older or presentation videos |
| `1:1` | Square videos, like ones made for social media |

Match the video’s own shape, so the player doesn’t show bars. The poster and the player fill the same box, so nothing moves when the player replaces the poster.

## States

The poster is the button. The play button on it follows its state.

| State | What changes | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| Default | The poster, with the play button centered on a scrim | `--verity-color-background-scrim`, `--verity-control-size-xl`, `--verity-icon-size-lg` |
| Hover | A light fill on the play button, over its scrim. Devices with a real pointer only. | `--verity-color-background-transparent-medium` |
| Focus | The shared focus ring around the whole poster, following its corners, and the play button’s hover fill. Keyboard focus only. | `--verity-focus-ring-width`, `--verity-focus-ring-offset`, `--verity-color-border-focus` |
| Active | A stronger fill on the play button while pressed | `--verity-color-background-transparent-strong` |
| Disabled | None: a video can always be played | — |

## Space

| What | Token |
|---|---|
| Corners, of the poster and the player | `--verity-border-radius-md` |
| The play button | `--verity-control-size-xl` (64), with a `--verity-icon-size-lg` (32) icon |

It fills its container’s width and adds no space outside itself.
