# MediaBlock

A picture with an optional caption below it. It’s a figure, so screen readers connect the caption to what it describes.

<Canvas of={MediaBlockStories.Default} />

## When to use

- **For media that belongs to the content,** like a screenshot of a design or a photo from research, with or without a caption.
- **With a caption,** when the picture needs a sentence of context: what it shows, or why it matters.
- **In a Gallery,** for several pictures shown together. Each item in a Gallery is a MediaBlock.
- **Not for a decorative picture** that doesn’t belong to the content. Use an `Image` on its own, with `alt=""`.

## Accessibility

A MediaBlock renders a `<figure>`, with the caption in a `<figcaption>`. Screen readers announce the figure and read its caption as part of it.

| Situation | What to do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Writing the caption | Add context the picture can’t give on its own. Don’t repeat the image’s `alt`. | “Step 2 asked for a bank login with no explanation.” |
| The picture has a caption | Still write the Image’s `alt`, describing what’s in the picture. | `alt="The setup screen, on step 2 of 5"` |
| It’s a long explanation | Keep the caption to a sentence or two, and put the rest in the text around it. | A paragraph in `Prose` before the MediaBlock |

## Media

The media is the MediaBlock’s `children`: an `Image`, an `Animation`, or a `Video`. The media fills the MediaBlock’s width, and the MediaBlock fills whatever holds it. See **Media › Animation** and **Media › Video**.

## Caption

The caption sits under the media in the body-default text style (`--verity-viewport-typography-body-default-*`) and `--verity-color-content-subtle`, so it reads as a note on the picture, quieter than the text around it. It’s never wider than the content column (`--verity-content-column-width`, 880), so under wide media it stays a comfortable line length instead of stretching across the page. The caption block is centered, and its text is aligned left, so under wide media it starts at the same edge as the text above it.

<Canvas of={MediaBlockStories.LongCaption} />

## Width

In a `Section` or `Container`, every block sits in the content column, `--verity-content-column-width` (880). Use `width="wide"` to break out to the full width, `--verity-content-wide-width` (1824), for a screenshot or image that needs room to show its detail. Inside another component, it fills the space it’s given. See **Layout › Container**.

## Space

The caption sits `--verity-spacing-xs` (8–16) below the media, close enough to read as part of it. A MediaBlock adds no space outside itself; the `Section` or `Gallery` it’s in sets the space around it.
