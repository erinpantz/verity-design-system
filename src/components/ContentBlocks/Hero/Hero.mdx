# Hero

The band at the top of a page: a large headline, a short intro, and up to two actions that say where to go next.

<Canvas of={HeroStories.Default} />

## When to use

- **At the top of a page that introduces something,** with one or two places to go next, or none when the page itself shows where to go, as on Home.
- **Once per page.** Its headline is the page’s `<h1>`.
- **Not with a CaseStudyHeader.** Its heading is also the page’s `<h1>`, so a page starts with a Hero or a CaseStudyHeader, never both.
- **Not for a call to action partway down a page.** Use a `Section` with a `Button`.

## Accessibility

A Hero renders a `<header>`: the page’s introduction. Its headline is the page’s `<h1>`, in the `display-default` text style, which is larger than `heading-1`. Its actions are Buttons with `href`, so each one renders a link (`<a>`) that looks like a button.

| Situation | What to do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Writing the headline | Say what the page is about in one short sentence, in sentence case. It’s the page’s title for screen readers. | “I design tools that give people their time back” |
| Writing the actions | Start each label with a verb that says where it goes. Put the main one in `primaryAction`. | “View my work,” then “Book a call” |
| Only one thing to do | Leave out `secondaryAction`. | — |
| Choosing a background | Pick any of the four. The text and buttons follow it, and meet the 4.5:1 contrast ratio in both themes. | `background="brand"` |

Every focus ring follows the background, not the button, so it keeps a 3:1 contrast ratio on every background in both themes. Some buttons’ colors don’t match the band. For example, on knockout the primary button is default, so Hero sets its ring to `--verity-color-border-focus-knockout`.

## Background

| `background` | Fill | Headline | Intro | Primary button | Secondary button | Focus ring |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `default` | None: the page shows through, which is `--verity-color-background-default` in `Shell`. Moving media passes behind the page (see Media). | `--verity-color-content-default` | `--verity-color-content-subtle` | `default` | `default` | `--verity-color-border-focus` |
| `subtle` | `--verity-color-background-subtle` | `--verity-color-content-default` | `--verity-color-content-subtle` | `default` | `default` | `--verity-color-border-focus` |
| `knockout` | `--verity-color-background-knockout` | `--verity-color-content-knockout` | `--verity-color-content-knockout` | `default` | `knockout` | `--verity-color-border-focus-knockout` |
| `brand` | `--verity-color-background-brand` | `--verity-color-content-default` | `--verity-color-content-subtle` | `brand` | `default` | `--verity-color-border-focus-brand` |

The intro is quieter than the headline on `default`, `subtle`, and `brand`; on `knockout` both use the knockout text color. The intro is `body-xl` (28 on Large and XL, 24 on Medium and Small), large text at every size, so it needs 3:1: subtle on the Red brand band is about 3.05:1, the closest pair. Button has no brand secondary, so on `brand` the secondary button is `default`. On `subtle`, the primary button’s fill (brand 700) is close to the band’s color (brand 800), so the button’s edge is faint; its label still passes.

Use `default` on most pages, as on Home with its Reel, `subtle` for a quieter band, and `knockout` or `brand` to make a page’s opening stand out.

## Width

| `width` | The band | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| `column` | The default. The content column, like the blocks below it | Most pages |
| `wide` | Breaks out to `--verity-content-wide-width` (1824) in a `Section` or `Container`, like a `MediaBlock` with `width="wide"` | When the band should line up with wide content below it, as on Home |

Only the band goes wide. The headline and intro are one group, never wider than `--verity-content-column-width` (880), so the text keeps a readable measure either way, and it sits at the start, with the buttons. On Small and Medium, where the column and the wide width are the same, the two look the same.

## Actions

Both are optional. `primaryAction` is the main one; `secondaryAction` sits next to it and needs a `primaryAction`. Both are `xl` Buttons, side by side. On a narrow screen, the second one wraps below the first.

With no `primaryAction`, there are no actions at all, and no space for them: the band ends after the text. Home uses its Hero this way, because the cards below it are where to go next.

## Media

`media` is optional: something beside the text, like the `Reel` on Home or a photo on Profile. Without it, the Hero is text only.

With it, the headline, intro, and actions stay one group, the body, on the left, still at most `--verity-content-column-width` wide, and the media sits at the end of the row, at least `--verity-spacing-lg` away, vertically centered against the text, with its own contents centered. The two share a row that wraps:

- **The body** is at least `--verity-hero-text-min-width` wide (460 on XL and Large, 300 on Medium, 200 on Small), never wider than the Hero, and fills the rest of the row.
- **The media** is at most `--verity-hero-media-width` wide (560 on XL, 440 on Large, 260 on Medium, 280 on Small), and never wider than the Hero. Beside the text, it can give up width, down to `--verity-hero-media-min-width` (320 on Large; the same as its widest on XL, Medium, and Small), before it wraps. When there’s room, the media reaches its widest before the text gets any wider. What’s in it fills it and keeps its aspect ratio, like an `Image` or a `Reel`. A `Reel`’s size, `--verity-reel-size`, points to `--verity-hero-media-width`, so a Reel and a photo in a Hero are always the same size. It sits at the end of the row, against the band’s padding. When the body has grown as wide as it gets, the space left over goes between the text and the media, never after the media.
- **When the text at its narrowest, the gap, and the media at its narrowest don’t fit,** the media wraps under the text, at the start, with `--verity-spacing-lg` between them, and is up to `--verity-hero-media-width` again. No breakpoint decides it: the wrap does.

When the media sits beside the text, for a wide Hero in `Shell` (measured in **Layout › Shell › Home** for `default`, and the With media and With a photo stories for a band, which wrap at the same widths). A band’s padding leaves less room, so on a band the media moves beside the text at a wider screen:

| Screen | Media | Beside the text, on `default` | Beside the text, on a band |
|---|---|---|---|
| XL (1600 and up) | 560 | Always | Always |
| Large (960–1599) | 440, shrinking toward 320 before it wraps | Always: 440 from 1076px wide, shrinking to 324 at 960 | From 1116px wide: 440 from 1236px, shrinking to 320 at 1116 |
| Medium (560–959) | 260 | From 672px wide | From 768px wide |
| Small (320–559) | 280, or the Hero’s width when it’s narrower | Never: always under the text | Never: always under the text |

Below those widths on each screen, it wraps under the text.

The media comes after the text in the reading order, and the Hero is still the page’s `<header>` with its `<h1>`.

**The text is always on top.** The body and the media each have their own stacking layer, and the body’s is above the media’s, on every background. So media that moves, like a Reel’s turning cards, passes behind the text and never covers it.

**A visible band clips.** On `subtle`, `knockout`, and `brand`, the band clips anything that moves past its edges, keeping its rounded corners, so a Reel’s cards slide out of sight at the band’s edge and the page never scrolls sideways. On `default` there’s no band to clip to, so the media opens out across the page instead (next).

**On `default`, moving media passes behind the page.** A Reel’s cards swing out freely, behind everything else on the page: MainNav, the content after the Hero, the Footer, ThemeToggle, and BackLink, as well as the Hero’s own text. The Hero has no fill there, and it sets `--media-moving-layer` for what’s in its media, which Reel reads to put its cards behind the page. `Shell` keeps the page one layer, so the cards stay above its background, and clips them sideways at the window’s edges, so the page never scrolls sideways, while they can still rise toward the nav.

- **Nothing they pass behind is blocked.** The cards never take the pointer, so links and buttons they pass behind work as usual. What stays put, like a Reel’s pause button, stays above the page, and hovering over or focusing the Reel still holds it.
- **The nav has no background,** so a passing card shows through behind its text, dimmed by the Reel’s scrim. That’s intended.
- **Only in `Shell`.** Shell offers the layer behind the page, keeps the cards above its background, and clips them at the window’s sides. Outside it, the cards stay in the media’s own layer, above whatever is behind the Hero, and the Hero doesn’t clip them, so whatever holds the Hero needs to clip them at the window’s sides, or the page scrolls sideways (Hero’s stories do this for themselves). See it in **Layout › Shell › Home**.

## Space

A Hero fills the width it’s given and adds no space outside itself. On `subtle`, `knockout`, and `brand`, its padding is there for the band, on every side. On `default`, there’s no band to see: the padding is on the top and bottom only, to give the page’s opening more room, and the sides have none, so the text lines up with the column.

| Space | Token |
|---|---|
| Inside, on `subtle`, `knockout`, and `brand` | `--verity-padding-lg` on every side |
| Inside, on `default` | `--verity-padding-lg` on the top and bottom; `--verity-padding-none` on the sides |
| Between the headline and the intro, inside the text group | `--verity-spacing-sm` |
| Between the text group and the actions | `--verity-spacing-lg` |
| Between the body and the media, side by side or wrapped | `--verity-spacing-lg` |
| The body’s narrowest width beside media | `--verity-hero-text-min-width` (460, 460, 300, 200) |
| The media’s widest | `--verity-hero-media-width` (560, 440, 260, 280), and never wider than the Hero. `--verity-reel-size` points to it. |
| The media’s narrowest beside the text | `--verity-hero-media-min-width` (560, 320, 260, 280): below this, it wraps |
| Between the buttons | `--verity-spacing-xs` |
| Corners | `--verity-border-radius-md`, always (Verity has no edge-to-edge layouts) |
| Text | The headline in `display-default`; the intro in `body-xl` |

## In Figma

| Figma | Code |
|---|---|
| Component `Hero` | `<Hero />` |
| Property `Background` | `background`. `default` is the default in both. |
| Property `Border radius` (one option, `md`) | No prop: always `md`. Figma keeps the property because it can’t delete a variant property. |
| Property `Heading` | `heading` |
| Property `Intro` | `intro` |
| Property `Show actions` | Not a prop: set `primaryAction` to show the actions, or leave it out for none |
| Property `Primary action` (an exposed Button) | `primaryAction`: its label and where it goes. Hero sets the Button’s variant, size, and color. |
| Property `Secondary action` (an exposed Button) | `secondaryAction` |
| Property `Show secondary action` | Not a prop: set `secondaryAction` to show it, or leave it out |
| Property `Width` | `width`. `column` is the default in both. |
| Frame `Body` | The headline, intro, and actions, as one group: at least `hero/text-min-width`, at most `content/column-width` |
| The media beside Body | `media`, any content, like a `Reel` or an `Image`. In Figma, an Image’s width is set to `hero/media-width`; in code, the media is at most that wide, and an Image fills it. |
| Frame `Text` | The headline and intro group: `spacing/sm` between them, at most `content/column-width` wide |
| The fixed width | Not in code: the Hero fills the width it’s given, the column or the wide width |
