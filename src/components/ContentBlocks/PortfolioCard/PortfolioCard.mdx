# PortfolioCard

A link to one piece of work, for the Portfolio and Home pages: the heading, the project and years, a label for the type that looks like a button, and its pictures. The whole card is one link.

<Canvas of={PortfolioCardStories.Default} />

## When to use

- **On the Portfolio and Home pages,** one card per case study or brand gallery.
- **Several together:** stack them, or put them in a wide `Grid` with `itemWidth="xl"`, so the large headings have room: two to a row on a wide screen. Each card lays itself out by its own width.
- **Not for a link inside a page’s text.** Use `Link`.
- **Not for a call to action.** Use `Button`: a PortfolioCard always goes to a piece of work.

## Accessibility

A PortfolioCard renders one `<a href>` around everything, so the whole card is one stop for the keyboard and one link for screen readers.

- **Its name is the heading** (`aria-labelledby`), so a screen reader hears “Designing AI that care teams trust, link,” not everything inside the card.
- **The project, years, and type describe it** (`aria-describedby`), read after the name: “Laguna Companion, 2024–2025, Case study.”
- **The type label looks like a button but isn’t one:** it’s a plain `<span>` (`Button` with `as="span"`), because the whole card is already the link, and a link can’t hold another link or button. It has no focus of its own, and it’s read as part of the description.
- **The pictures are decorative** (`alt=""`): the heading already says where the link goes, and descriptions of thumbnails would make the link’s name long.
- **The heading is a real `<h2>` or `<h3>`,** so people who move by headings find each card. `headingLevel` sets it: 2 on a page of cards, 3 under a Section’s heading.

| Situation | What to do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Writing the heading | The case study’s own heading, or the brand’s name for a gallery. Sentence case, no period. | “Designing AI that care teams trust” |
| Writing the year | The years of the work, with an en dash between two years | “2024–2025” |
| Choosing the heading level | 2 when the cards are the page’s main sections, 3 when they sit under a Section’s heading | `headingLevel={3}` under “Selected work” |

## Type

| `type` | Pictures | Label |
|---|---|---|
| `case-study` | The default. One `thumbnail`, at 3:2. Shows the `project`. | “Case study” |
| `brand-gallery` | Exactly three `thumbnails`, at 1:1, in one row | “Brand gallery” |

The label looks exactly like a secondary `Button` (`color="default"`, size `md`) with the `forward` arrow at its end, so the card reads as something to open. It follows the card’s state: the button’s hover look while the card is hovered or focused, and its active look while it’s pressed. It never shows a focus ring of its own; the card has it.

## Layout

The card follows its own width, not the screen’s: the text and the pictures sit side by side, and the pictures wrap below the text when there isn’t room for both minimums.

| Part | Case study | Brand gallery |
|---|---|---|
| Text | At least `--verity-grid-item-min-width-md` (280), at most `--verity-content-column-width` (880) | At least `--verity-grid-item-min-width-md` (280), at most `--verity-portfolio-card-text-max-width` (400) |
| Pictures | At least `--verity-grid-item-min-width-lg` (400; 300 on Medium and Small) | The rest of the card, with no maximum: at least `--verity-grid-item-min-width-lg`. The three thumbnails always stay in one row and shrink together, `--verity-spacing-md` apart. |

Side by side, a case study’s text and picture share the width equally; a brand gallery’s text stops at 400 and the thumbnails take the rest.

## Size

| `size` | Use for | Heading | Layout |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lg` | The default. A card on its own row, wide | `display-sm` | Text on the left, pictures on the right, wrapping below when narrow (see **Layout**) |
| `sm` | Several side by side, like three in a `Grid` with `itemWidth="md"` and `fillRows` | `title-lg` (28/36 on Medium and Small) | Picture and text, each at least `--verity-portfolio-card-content-min-width` (180): side by side, picture on the left, when both fit; stacked, picture on top, when they don’t |

In a small card, the wrap decides the layout, with no breakpoint: a narrow card stacks, a wider one puts its picture beside its text. A case study’s 3:2 picture fills its side; a brand gallery’s three thumbnails stay square, in one row, and its text has no maximum width.

**The picture comes first on screen, the heading first in the code.** The picture is decorative and the heading names the link, so the heading stays first for screen readers; CSS moves the picture before it, and nothing focusable moves.

**Labels line up across a row.** When a Grid row stretches a small card taller than its content, the extra height goes above the label, side by side or stacked, so the labels sit at the same height across the row.

Everything else is the same as `lg`: colors, the button-look label, focus ring, lift, growth, image zoom, motion, and reduce motion.

## States

| State | What changes | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| Default | The brand fill and border, the default text color, and a small shadow | `--verity-color-background-brand`, `--verity-color-border-brand`, `--verity-color-content-default`, `--verity-shadow-sm-*` |
| Hover | The card rises and grows a little, with a large shadow; a lighter fill and border, brighter text, and the pictures zoom. Devices with a real pointer only, so a tap on a phone doesn’t leave a card raised. | `--verity-motion-lift-md` (8), `--verity-motion-scale-hover` (1.02), `--verity-shadow-lg-*`, `--verity-color-background-brand-hover`, `--verity-color-border-brand-hover`, `--verity-color-content-default-hover` |
| Focus | The hover look, raised and grown, plus the shared focus ring outside the card, following its corners. The ring moves and grows with the card. Keyboard focus only, on every device. | `--verity-focus-ring-width`, `--verity-focus-ring-offset`, `--verity-color-border-focus` |
| Label | Follows the card: the secondary Button’s default look at rest, its hover look on the card’s hover and focus, and its active look on press. Paired that way, it reaches 4.5:1 in both themes; the closest is Red’s hover, at 4.63:1. | `--verity-button-secondary-color-*` |
| Active | The card settles back down, with no lift or growth and the small shadow, so pressing reads as pushing it in. A darker fill and border, and brighter text. The pictures stay zoomed, so they don’t jump back. | `--verity-color-background-brand-active`, `--verity-color-border-brand-active`, `--verity-shadow-sm-*` |
| Visited | No change: a card looks the same once visited | — |
| Disabled | None: a card always goes somewhere | — |

## Motion

| What | How | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| Fill, border, text color, and shadow | Fade between states | `--verity-motion-duration-fast`, `--verity-motion-easing-standard` |
| The lift and growth | The card moves up and grows on hover and focus, and settles back on mouse out, blur, or press | `--verity-motion-lift-md`, `--verity-motion-scale-hover`, `--verity-motion-duration-playful`, `--verity-motion-easing-standard` |
| The zoom | Each picture grows to 105% inside its own frame, clipped by the frame’s rounded corners, and eases back on mouse out | `--verity-portfolio-card-image-scale-hover` (1.05), `--verity-motion-duration-playful`, `--verity-motion-easing-standard` |
| Reduce motion is on | No lift, growth, or zoom. The color and shadow fades stay, since they don’t move anything. | — |

The lift, growth, and zoom move together at the same pace, with the standard easing, not the playful one: motion that bounces looks jittery.

Raised, a card grows about 1% on each side and moves up 8px, so in a stack with `gap="md"` it stays clear of the card above. Nothing around the card clips it or its shadow.

## Space

| Space | Token |
|---|---|
| Inside the card | `--verity-padding-lg`; `--verity-padding-md` for `sm` |
| Between the text and the pictures, side by side or stacked | `--verity-spacing-lg`; `--verity-spacing-md` for `sm` |
| Between the heading and the details, and between the details and the label | `--verity-spacing-sm` |
| Between the project and the year | `--verity-spacing-xxs` |
| Between a brand gallery’s thumbnails | `--verity-spacing-md`; `--verity-spacing-sm` for `sm` |
| Between the project and the year, `sm` | None, as in Figma |
| The narrowest the picture or the text gets, `sm` | `--verity-portfolio-card-content-min-width` (180) |
| Corners and border | `--verity-border-radius-md`, `--verity-border-width-sm` |

It adds no space outside itself: the stack or Grid it’s in sets that.

## Color

The heading in `display-sm` and the details in `body-default` use `--verity-color-content-default`, then `--verity-color-content-default-hover` on hover, focus, and press. Every pair passes 4.5:1 in both themes; the closest is the hover fill in Red, at about 4.6:1.

Each brand border is one step lighter than its fill, so the card’s edge shows on the page’s dark background.
