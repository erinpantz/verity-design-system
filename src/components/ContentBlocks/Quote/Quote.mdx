# Quote

A quote set apart from the text around it, with who said it: a testimonial, or something a customer said in research.

<Canvas of={QuoteStories.Default} />

## When to use

- **Between blocks in a Section,** to let one person’s words make a point, like a finding from research or a word from a client.
- **Several quotes side by side** go in a `Grid` with `gap="lg"`, so each one reads as its own. See **Layout › Grid**.
- **Not for a quote inside running text.** In a `Prose` block, use a blockquote (`>` in Markdown). It has the same rule and text style.
- **Not for a heading or a pull quote of your own words.** Use `Heading` or `TextBlock`.

## Accessibility

A Quote renders a `<figure>`: the quote is a `<blockquote>`, and who said it is the figure’s `<figcaption>`. Screen readers announce the quote as a quote, with the attribution as its caption.

| Situation | What to do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Writing the quote | Leave out the quotation marks. Quote adds the curly marks “ ” around `quote`. | `I just want to get through checkout.` |
| A quote inside the quote | Use single curly marks ‘ ’ around it. | `They called it the ‘slow lane’ at checkout.` |
| The person can’t be named | Give a description instead of a name. `name` is required, so a quote is never unattributed. | `name="Interview participant"` |
| Showing their role | Use `title`. It follows the name after a comma. | `title="Head of product, Basket"` |

The dash before the name is only for looks, so screen readers skip it.

## Title

| `title` | What it shows | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Left out | The default. A dash and the name. | A participant, or anyone whose role doesn’t matter |
| Set | A dash, the name, a comma, and the title | A client or colleague, whose role gives the quote weight |

## Space

Quote fills the width it’s given and adds no space outside itself. Whatever holds it, like a Section, sets the space around it.

| Space | Token |
|---|---|
| The rule beside the quote | `--verity-border-width-xl`, in `--verity-color-background-brand` |
| Between the rule and the text | `--verity-spacing-md` |
| Between the quote and who said it | `--verity-spacing-sm` |
| Between the dash and the name | `--verity-spacing-xs` |
| Text | The quote in the `blockquote` text style; who said it in `label-default` |

The rule and the space beside it are the same as a Prose blockquote’s, so the two match on a page.
