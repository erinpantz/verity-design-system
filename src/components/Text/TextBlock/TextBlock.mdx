# TextBlock

A heading with what it introduces below it: a few short paragraphs, or a component such as a list or a Grid of stat cards. Its heading is level 3 by default, for use inside a Section.

<Canvas of={TextBlockStories.Default} />

## When to use

- **For short copy with its own heading,** such as “The problem” in a project summary, or each item in a Grid of outcomes.
- **For one to three short paragraphs.** Each paragraph is separated by the same gap as the heading and the body.
- **For a heading that introduces a component,** such as “Takeaways” above an `UnorderedList`, or “Outcomes” above a `Grid` of `StatCard`s. Put the component in the body, so the heading sits `--verity-spacing-sm` from it. A heading at the end of a `Prose` block would sit a Section’s block gap away instead. See **Guidelines › Spacing and rhythm**.
- **Not for long-form text,** like a case study write-up. Use `Prose`, where each block sets the space above it.
- **Not for a heading on its own.** Use `Heading`.

## Accessibility

A TextBlock renders a `<div>` with a `Heading` and the body text in paragraphs (`<p>`). The heading is a real heading, so it appears in the page’s outline.

| Situation | What to do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| It’s inside a Section | Keep the default, level 3, one level below the Section’s heading. | “The problem” inside a “Research and discovery” Section |
| It’s directly under the page title, with no Section | Use `headingLevel={2}`. Never skip a level. | A short page with no sections |
| The body has more than one paragraph | Write each paragraph as a `<p>`, so screen readers can move between them. | Two paragraphs about the problem |

## Heading level

| `headingLevel` | Element | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| `3` | `<h3>` | The default: a TextBlock inside a Section |
| `2` | `<h2>` | A TextBlock that starts a topic on its own |
| `1` | `<h1>` | Rarely: only when the TextBlock is the page’s title and intro |

The heading’s text style follows its level; see **Text › Heading** for the sizes.

## Space

TextBlock adds no space outside itself, and it’s no wider than the text width, `--verity-text-max-width` (680). Inside, the space between the heading and the body, and between paragraphs, is `--verity-spacing-sm` (16–24, growing on larger screens).

## Color

The heading and the body both use `--verity-color-content-default`. The body uses the body-default text style (`--verity-viewport-typography-body-default-*`). A component in the body keeps its own text styles.
